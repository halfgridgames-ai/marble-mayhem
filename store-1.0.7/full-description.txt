Roll, bump and boost your way to the finish line in Marble Mayhem: Downhill Dash, the marble racing game where eight marbles fight for first place on wild downhill tracks.

RACE DOWNHILL
Steer your marble through loops, jumps, glass tubes, kelp flumes, haunted mansions and sky castles. 41 hand-built courses, plus a Random Course that builds a brand new track every time you play.

WIN GRAND PRIX CUPS
12 four-race cups, from the Toy Box Cup to the Cloud Kingdom Cup. Score points in every race and finish in the top three to take home the trophy.

BANANAS, BEES AND MORE
Grab item boxes for 10 power-ups: drop or throw Bananas, send a homing Bumblebee after the leader, catch up with the Magnet, or blast rivals with Rockets, Shockwaves and Lightning. Land a hit and earn bonus coins.

WIN IN STYLE
Finish on the podium for a slow-motion finish, confetti cannons and a victory dance.

DAILY MISSIONS
Three fresh missions every day. Complete them all for a free Mystery Box.

SURVIVAL MODE
The rings of the funnel crumble away one by one. Bump your rivals off the edge and be the last marble rolling.

RACE YOUR FRIENDS ONLINE
Create a room, share the code and race live, or go head to head in a 1v1 room.

COLLECT 29 MARBLES
From rare to mythic, with riders like Captain Quack. Unlock new characters by playing, open mystery boxes, and upgrade your steering, speed and bumping.

FEATURES
• Quick races, perfect for a few minutes of fun
• Simple one-thumb joystick controls
• Top times for every course
• Daily rewards that grow with your streak
• Play offline (online races need a connection)

Marble Mayhem is free to play, with ads and optional in-app purchases.
