# Marble Mayhem

Downhill marble racing for phone and desktop: nudge your marble, grab risky item boxes, race Grand Prix cups and unlock special marbles.

Play: https://halfgridgames-ai.github.io/marble-mayhem/

## Install on your phone
- **iPhone (Safari):** open the link, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android (Chrome):** open the link, tap the ⋮ menu, then **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).

The installed app opens fullscreen and works offline after the first load.

## Controls
- Phone: joystick (right) to steer and nudge, BUMP (left), tap the item slot to use an item.
- Keyboard: arrows to steer/nudge/brake, Space to bump, E for items, C for camera, M for music.
